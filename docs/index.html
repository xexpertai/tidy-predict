How to read this: the forecast gives a likely range. Most weeks should land inside it. “Keep for a busy week” is the top of that range, so you rarely run out.

What do the technical names mean?

Likely range = P10 to P90. P10: sales fall below this in about 1 week out of 10. P90: sales go above this in about 1 week out of 10. So about 8 weeks out of 10 land in between.

Expected = the middle forecast (P50, the median). Keep for a busy week = P90.

Actually sold is what the shop really sold that week, from the same public file. The forecast never saw it.

The small line shows weekly units sold, 5 Sep to 21 Nov 2011 (12 full weeks before the forecast); each line is scaled to its own product, so compare shapes, not heights. Wide-range note: shown when P90 is at least 8 times P10.
